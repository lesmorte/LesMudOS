Legends of Hollowmere now has a home on the web.

Here you can read the **Chronicle** of Hollowmere, the world's own record of its deeds, and see who stands in the **Hall of Heroes**. You can also look over the roads of Greenvale, learn what the five callings can become at the Hall of Legends in Oakenfell, and meet the factions that shape the region.

The game itself is still played in a MUD client or over telnet. Everything you need to begin, step by step, is on the [How to play](/play) page.

The Chronicle and the Hall of Heroes are written by the game, not by us. They fill in as adventurers make their marks, and they refresh about once a minute.

News will appear here as the world grows.
