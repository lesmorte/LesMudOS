# City Design Document: Oakenfell

## Overview

Oakenfell is the largest city in Greenvale and serves as the first major city encountered by most adventurers.

It is not the capital of Hollowmere.

It is not the largest city in the world.

Oakenfell is important because it is where ordinary people become adventurers and where adventurers begin carving legends.

For many players, Oakenfell should feel like the moment their journey expands beyond the safety of Vincent's Hollow.

The city serves as:

- Greenvale's trade center
- Regional government center
- Home of the Hall of Legends
- Hub for class advancement
- Banking center
- Transportation hub
- Faction crossroads
- Introduction to regional politics

## Design Philosophy

Oakenfell should feel lived-in.

It should not be a city built solely for players.

Citizens should have homes.

Tradesmen should have businesses.

Roads should lead somewhere.

Districts should have history.

The city should feel like it existed long before the player arrived.

## Population

Estimated population: 8,000 - 12,000 permanent residents.

Transient population: 1,000 - 3,000 travelers, merchants, and adventurers.

This makes Oakenfell significant without feeling like a giant metropolis.

## City Motto

"From Strong Roots Grow Great Things"

The phrase references the ancient oak from which the city derives its name.

## History

Nearly four centuries ago, a group of settlers established a small riverside village near a massive oak tree.

The oak dwarfed every other tree in Greenvale and was visible for miles across the countryside.

Travelers referred to it simply as: The Great Oak.

The tree became a meeting place, wayfinder marker, and symbol of safety.

As the settlement grew, the tree remained at its center.

During a terrible storm generations later, lightning struck the massive oak.

The tree burned for three days before eventually collapsing.

Rather than abandoning the symbol that had guided them, the settlers rebuilt around it.

The settlement became known as Oakenfell.

Over centuries the village grew into Greenvale's largest city.

## Layout

Oakenfell is built around five primary districts.

### Founder's District

The original settlement. Oldest part of the city.

Contains: Founder's Square, historic homes, City Hall, government buildings, ancient stone streets.

Atmosphere: traditional, respectable, historic.

### Market District

Economic heart of Greenvale.

Contains: merchant stalls, permanent storefronts, caravan offices, auction houses, banks.

Atmosphere: busy, crowded, noisy, colorful.

### River District

Located along the River Mere.

Contains: docks, fishing operations, warehouses, shipwrights, river trade offices.

Atmosphere: industrial, working-class, practical.

### Guild Quarter

Home to organized professions.

Contains: Hall of Legends, crafting guilds, trainers, libraries, archives.

Atmosphere: aspirational, scholarly, ambitious.

### Southgate District

Newest section of Oakenfell.

Contains: inns, stables, traveler housing, mercenary lodges, adventurer services.

Atmosphere: rough but welcoming.

## City Landmarks

### Hall of Legends

The most important player-facing structure.

Purpose: Advanced Class Selection, Guild Representatives, Chronicle Access, Renown Records, Faction Contacts.

Architectural theme: massive timber and stone structure, designed to look like many buildings seamlessly joined over generations.

The Hall should feel prestigious but accessible.

### Founder's Square

The civic center of Oakenfell.

Contains: public notices, seasonal events, speeches, festivals, minor markets.

A gathering place for players and NPCs.

### Assembly Hall

Meeting place of the Greenvale Assembly.

Contains: political quests, regional governance content, faction storylines.

Most players will not initially understand how influential this building is.

### Greenvale Market

Largest public market in Greenvale.

Contains: vendors, crafters, auctions, resource exchange.

Should feel constantly active.

### The Silver Stag Inn

Oakenfell's most famous inn.

Acts as: rumor source, quest starter location, meeting place, social hub.

Many adventures begin here.

## The Hidden Grove

### Design Intent

One of the most important locations in the city.

Most new players should never discover it immediately.

Many longtime citizens are unaware it exists.

Those who find it often return.

### Location

Hidden behind old walls and overgrown pathways within the Founder's District.

There is no obvious sign.

Access requires observation, exploration, or local knowledge.

Multiple secret entrances may exist.

### History

The Hidden Grove occupies the original location of the Great Oak.

When the oak fell centuries ago, city leaders attempted to preserve the site.

As Oakenfell expanded, newer construction gradually obscured the old grounds.

Over generations the grove faded from public memory.

A small number of caretakers quietly maintained it.

Today it exists as one of Oakenfell's oldest surviving places.

### Features

The grove is peaceful.

Birdsong replaces city noise.

Ancient moss-covered stones line the pathways.

The centerpiece is the massive stump of the Great Oak.

### The Great Stump

The stump should be awe-inspiring.

Diameter: approximately 40-60 feet. Height: 8-12 feet.

The roots extend across much of the grove.

The wood has petrified over centuries.

Visitors can see: lightning scars, ancient carvings, memorial plaques, historical markers.

The stump serves as a physical connection to Oakenfell's origins.

### Cultural Significance

Many traditions originate here.

Some citizens leave offerings.

Children tell stories about it.

Couples make vows nearby.

Retired adventurers occasionally visit.

Local legends claim:

- The roots still live.
- Spirits gather here.
- Druids can hear the tree whisper.
- Hidden passages exist beneath the stump.

No official explanation confirms or denies these rumors.

### Gameplay Purposes

The Hidden Grove should support: exploration content, lore discovery, Druid questlines, future secrets, prestige class content, Chronicle-related content, seasonal events.

The location should remain relevant for the life of the game.

## Faction Presence

| Faction | Presence |
|---|---|
| Greenvale Assembly | Headquarters present. Strong influence. |
| Merchant Consortium | Major influence. Controls much of the city's commerce. |
| Hall of Legends | Major influence. Controls advancement and guild affairs. |
| Riverwatch Rangers | Moderate influence. Maintain a city office. |
| Thorn Pact | Officially absent. Unofficially active. |
| Circle of Ash | Publicly nonexistent. Secretly interested in Oakenfell's oldest locations. Particularly the Hidden Grove. |

## Player Experience Goals

When arriving from Vincent's Hollow players should feel: wonder, opportunity, scale, possibility.

Oakenfell should communicate that the world is much larger than the starting village.

Every road leaving the city should suggest future adventures.

Every district should contain stories waiting to be discovered.

The city marks the end of the tutorial phase of life in Hollowmere.

For many adventurers it will always feel like home.

For every legend recorded in the Chronicle, the journey began somewhere.

For most, it began in Vincent's Hollow.

But their legend truly began in Oakenfell.
