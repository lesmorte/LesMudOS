# Factions of GreenvaleExpanded Faction Reputation System

## Purpose

Faction Reputation is a secondary progression system.

Character Level measures personal power.

Faction Reputation measures influence, trust, and standing within Hollowmere.

A player's faction relationships should shape their story almost as much as their class selection.

---

# Reputation Tracking

Every major faction maintains independent standing.

Examples:

Greenvale Assembly: Friendly

Riverwatch Rangers: Exalted

Merchant Consortium: Neutral

Thorn Pact: Suspicious

Circle of Ash: Unknown

A player can be simultaneously loved by one faction and disliked by another.

---

# Legendary Reputation

Legendary Reputation is intentionally difficult.

A player should not accidentally become Legendary.

Achieving Legendary status should require:

- Long-term commitment
- Numerous quests
- Faction loyalty
- Significant world contributions

Legendary members become living examples of that faction's ideals.

NPCs recognize them throughout Hollowmere.

Their names may appear in books, monuments, and future story content.

The goal is that players eventually become part of Hollowmere's lore rather than simply participants within it.

# Factions of Greenvale region

## Design Philosophy

The factions of Greenvale should represent competing interests rather than purely good or evil alignments.

Most citizens belong to, work for, or sympathize with one or more factions.

Players should be able to earn or lose reputation with factions through quests, actions, guild affiliations, and world decisions.

Faction relationships should influence:

- Quest availability
- Prices and merchants
- Guild opportunities
- Political storylines
- Access to restricted areas
- Prestige class opportunities
- Regional events

No faction should be entirely right or wrong.

---

# The Greenvale Assembly

## Motto

"Prosperity Through Unity"

## Headquarters

Assembly Hall, Oakenfall

## Type

Regional Government

### Description

The Greenvale Assembly governs most settled lands throughout Greenvale.

Originally formed by village leaders and merchants seeking mutual protection, the Assembly now serves as the political center of the region.

Most citizens view the Assembly favorably, though some believe it has become too comfortable and bureaucratic.

### Goals

- Maintain peace
- Protect trade routes
- Expand prosperity
- Prevent regional conflicts
- Preserve law and order

### Allies

- Merchant Consortium
- Hall of Legends
- Riverwatch Rangers

### Rivals

- The Thorn Pact
- Free Companies

# Renown System

# Renown System

## Purpose

Renown represents how widely known a character has become throughout Hollowmere.

Unlike faction reputation, Renown is global.

Reputation answers:

"What does this faction think of me?"

Renown answers:

"Have people heard of me?"

Renown should become one of the primary long-term progression systems in Hollowmere.

---

# Core Principles

Renown is:

- Global
- Character-specific
- Difficult to lose
- Earned through accomplishments
- Visible throughout the world

Renown is not:

- A currency
- A faction standing
- A replacement for level progression

Renown reflects the stories people tell about a character.

---

# Sources of Renown

Players gain Renown through meaningful achievements.

The goal is to reward memorable actions rather than repetitive grinding.

# Chronicle System

# The Chronicle System

## Purpose

The Chronicle is the official historical record of Hollowmere.

Its purpose is to preserve the stories, accomplishments, discoveries, and major events that shape the world.

The Chronicle transforms player achievements into world history.

Rather than simply completing content, players become part of the ongoing story of Hollowmere.

---

# Core Philosophy

Levels represent power.

Renown represents fame.

The Chronicle represents legacy.

A player may become powerful.

A player may become famous.

Only a select few become remembered.

---

# What Is The Chronicle?

The Chronicle is a living record maintained by the Hall of Legends.

It contains:

- Historical events
- Player accomplishments
- First discoveries
- Faction milestones
- Guild achievements
- Political events
- World boss victories
- Legendary item creations
- Regional histories

Every significant recorded event becomes part of Hollowmere's permanent history.

---

# Hall of Legends Integration

The Hall of Legends in Oakenfall houses the Grand Chronicle.

Players may:

- Browse historical entries
- Search notable figures
- View server-first accomplishments
- Read faction histories
- Review world events
- Research famous characters

The Chronicle should feel like a library, museum, and historical archive combined.

# Chronicle Value

The Chronicle exists to answer a single question:

"What happened here before I arrived?"

Every year Hollowmere should feel slightly different.

Every generation of players should leave something behind.

Many games allow players to consume content.

The Chronicle allows players to create history.

The ultimate goal is that years after launch, a new adventurer standing in the Hall of Legends can read the names of heroes, villains, explorers, rulers, crafters, and legends who helped shape the world they now inhabit.

A character's inventory may eventually disappear.

Their level may eventually be surpassed.

Their place in the Chronicle should endure.

# Event Signifigance Score

# Event Significance Score (ESS)

## Purpose

The Event Significance Score (ESS) determines how important an event is to the history of Hollowmere.

The score is used by multiple systems:

- Chronicle Entries
- Renown Awards
- NPC Dialogue
- World News
- Historical Books
- Monuments
- Statues
- World Changes
- Seasonal Recaps
- Hall of Heroes Inclusion
- Hall of Legends Inclusion

The system exists to answer one question:

"Did this event matter to the history of Hollowmere?"

---

# Core Philosophy

Not every event deserves to be remembered.

Thousands of monsters are defeated every day.

Countless quests are completed.

Only events with meaningful impact should become part of history.

The Event Significance Score acts as the world's filter.

The higher the score, the more likely the event becomes part of Hollowmere's permanent history.

---

# Score Range

Event Significance Scores range from:

0 to 10,000+

Most events should score below 100.

Only exceptional events should exceed 1,000.

World-defining events may exceed 5,000.

Legendary Event

Referenced in books.

May create physical world memorials.

Eligible for Hall of Heroes inclusion.

---

## 2,500 - 4,999

Realm-Shaping Event

Changes regional history.

Referenced for years.

May alter future content.

---

## 5,000+

World-Defining Event

Permanent Hall of Legends entry.

Historical books generated.

Monuments or statues may be created.

Future generations of NPCs reference the event.

Becomes part of official world lore.

---

# Event Decay

Event Significance never decreases.

Historical importance remains permanent.

However, recent events should receive greater visibility.

Example:

Yesterday's Dragon Kill appears in tavern rumors.

An event from three years ago appears primarily in Chronicle records.

Both remain equally historical.

---

# Shared Credit

Major events often involve multiple participants.

Event significance should be distributed.

Example:

Dragon Kill Score: 1,500

Party of Five

Each player receives:

1,500 ESS contribution credit

The event itself remains worth 1,500.

All participants are recorded.

The Chronicle should preserve every contributor.

---

# Dynamic Event Generation

Some events only become significant because of accumulated player actions.

Examples:

10,000 Goblins Killed Region-Wide

Road Finally Completed

Guild Controls Entire Trade Network

Player-Elected Assembly Member

Discovery of Hidden Continent

The system should allow significance to emerge naturally from gameplay.

---

---

---

ESS 5,000+

Hall of Legends Consideration

Permanent World History

---

# Design Goal

The Event Significance Score exists to ensure Hollowmere develops a genuine history.

Years after launch, players should be able to walk into the Hall of Legends, read the Chronicle, and discover events that were not scripted by developers but created by the actions of adventurers.

The greatest events in Hollowmere should not be written ahead of time.

They should emerge naturally from the players who inhabit the world.
