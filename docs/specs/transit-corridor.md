# Specification: the Greenvale Transit Corridor (owner's brief)

The owner's design for the road from Vincent's Hollow to Oakenfell, condensed from the source document without changing its content. Source: <https://claude.ai/artifact/XaBnAtoNWnXYRseunxjMG6>. How it is to be built is in [../PLAN-corridor-crafting.md](../PLAN-corridor-crafting.md).

## Overview

The Greenvale Transit Corridor is the primary overland route connecting Vincent's Hollow to Oakenfell. For new adventurers the journey is a major milestone. The road is approximately two days' travel for ordinary travelers and consists of roughly one hundred explorable locations. Most merchants, pilgrims, adventurers and officials traveling between the two use this route. A maintained road exists, but much of the surrounding Eldergreen Wood remains wild and dangerous. The route should feel safe enough to travel while still containing opportunities for adventure, exploration and danger.

## Design goals

- Introduce players to wilderness navigation
- Provide Level 5-10 content
- Reward exploration
- Teach players to leave the road
- Hide optional discoveries
- Introduce faction presence
- Establish Greenvale's scale
- Create a sense of progression toward civilization

The safest route should be suitable for Levels 5-7. More dangerous side trails should challenge Levels 8-10.

## Access: the Northgate pass

"At the southern edge of Vincent's Hollow stands the Northgate." The gate remains closed to ordinary citizens. Travel beyond requires the Greenvale Travel Pass, obtained by completing the first major storyline. Theme: the village recognizes the character as a trusted adventurer; receiving the pass symbolizes entry into the wider world.

## Secret dungeon 1: the Root Warrens

Recommended level 7-8. Access: a hollow beneath an uprooted tree near Heartwood; easy to miss. Theme: an extensive network of tunnels excavated beneath ancient roots, believed to have once housed goblins. Something else lives there now.

Creatures: Tunnel Rat (7), Goblin Tunneler (8), Root Creeper (8), Warren Broodmother (10).

Notable features: Goblin Blood supplies; early crafting materials; forgotten journals; future expansion hook.

## Secret dungeon 2: the Forgotten Barrow

Recommended level 9-10. Access: behind a collapsed stone shrine deep in Heartwood; only visible from an off-road trail. Theme: an ancient burial site predating Greenvale, one of the oldest locations in the region, with a connection to future lore.

Creatures: Restless Spirit (9), Ancient Skeleton (9), Barrow Guardian (10), Fallen Knight (10).

Notable features: the first strong lore connection to the Circle of Ash, lost civilizations and Hollowmere history.

## Faction presence

- **Riverwatch Rangers.** Maintain patrols. Locations: Riverwatch Outpost, roadside camps, travel checkpoints, hunter stations. Their presence keeps the road relatively safe. Players should occasionally encounter patrols.
- **Thorn Pact.** Subtle, mostly hidden. Evidence: strange markings, protected groves, ritual stones, animal behavior. Most new players will not understand their significance.
- **Circle of Ash.** Entirely secret, clues only: burned journals, arcane symbols, hidden caches, references in dungeon lore. No obvious faction members should appear yet.

## Travel experience

The journey should feel like a rite of passage. A player begins among farms and familiar faces, passes through increasingly wild lands, and encounters danger, mystery and history. Eventually the forest opens and the walls of Oakenfell rise above the trees. For many adventurers this is the first moment they realize how large Hollowmere is. By the time they enter Oakenfell they are no longer villagers. They are adventurers.
