## Canon for later writers

- **The older people.** One people, before Greenvale. Dark green-grey stone that is not local; blocks fitted without mortar, joints a hair wide; pointed arches and vaults, tall and narrow; carving interlaced in groups of seven; a procession of blank-faced figures; no letters anyone can read. Their three signs: a ring crossed by a bar; a single wavering line, like water; a ring with a gap in it, open to the north. They are given no name.
- **The Circle of Ash's emblem.** A ring broken at the top, a short bar across the break, three dots beneath. It is a different sign from the older people's three.
- **The closed ring** in the Barrow's last hall is the only closed ring the older people carved there. It is a clue whose meaning is the owner's to give.

- **The Fallen Knight** served a forerunner of the Thorn Pact, two or three centuries ago. The area shows this only by his thorn-shaped cloak pin.
- **Philippa Reeve**, the Consortium tally clerk at the Merchant Staging Grounds, is the bandits' informer. No text may reveal it before the quest that does. The bandits answer to an unnamed superior, "the Boss".
- **The Toll House and the Riverwatch** are overstretched, not corrupt.
- **The goblins of the road** are connected: families who fled the Root Warrens joined the raiders. What the scouts report to in the north is open.
- **The watchtower**: the Rangers know nothing of its cellar or its log. The garrison's fate and the well are open.
- **Morwenna's** fate is open. **"M."** of Hale's Acre is a mystery.
